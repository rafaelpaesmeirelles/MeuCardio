# Definitive UX principles — CorVIA

1. **Need first** — the system starts with what the physician needs to resolve now.
2. **Context second** — patient, disease, drug, exam, evidence or professional routine are contexts, not mandatory starting points.
3. **Universal Home** — consultório, hospital, emergência, diagnóstico, ensino, pesquisa and operational use all share the same Home.
4. **Search + Ask + Act** — discovery, AI reasoning and execution converge without becoming the same thing.
5. **CorVIA Intelligence ≠ Personal Assistant** — Intelligence connects knowledge; Personal Assistant accompanies the physician's professional day.
6. **Patient is important, not dominant** — Patient Command Center is rich only when a patient context exists.
7. **Everything with everything** — clinical entities expose real related entities and never artificial graph edges.
8. **Daily usefulness without patients** — scientific radar, knowledge, quick actions and professional routine keep Home valuable with zero registered patients.
9. **No fake smartness** — no fabricated traffic, alerts, counts, schedules, studies or regulatory updates.
10. **Premium means clarity** — visual sophistication cannot reduce legibility, speed, accessibility or clinical confidence.
